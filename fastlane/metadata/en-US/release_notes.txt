Thank you for using homeau.

- Move chores you couldn't get to onto tomorrow or another day. Use "Move to Tomorrow" or "Move to Today" from the chore menu, or pick a date from the chore details
- See unfinished chores from yesterday and earlier, all on Home. Days with unfinished chores are also marked on the chore board
- Check announcements from the homeau team with the bell on Home. You can choose whether to get announcement notifications in notification settings
- Other usability improvements and minor bug fixes

A little reward for everyday chores.
